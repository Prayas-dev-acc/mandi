Market Context
What Happened & Why It Matters
▸2024 shortage: India area fell to 390k ha (lowest in 5 yrs). Crop was weak → prices hit ₹23,176/q in Nov 2024 — a 15-year record.
▸2025 response: Farmers sowed a record 465k ha (+19%). Bumper harvest flooded mandis. Harvest price crashed to ₹3,590 (Apr 2025). Holding stock through storage season was a mistake — prices fell −14% from harvest to storage.
▸2026 sowing: 440k ha — still 16% above 10-yr average. But Oct 2025 had 119mm of rain (vs 27mm historical average) — severe waterlogging during sowing caused uneven germination and yield losses.
▸Weather damage confirmed by arrivals: Total mandi arrivals in the 2025-26 harvest season (Dec 2025–May 2026) were only 99,836 MT — 40% below the 2024-25 season and lowest since COVID. This is data we already have. Paper supply says ample; actual supply that reached mandi is tighter.
▸Now (May 2026): ₹5,260 near the 15-year median (₹5,100). South demand strong — Kerala ₹15,600. Storage season may hold better than model forecasts given tighter actual supply.
